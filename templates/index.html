      How a sentence becomes an action

      Five steps, all visible in the code. Nothing here calls an external AI service.

      1. Clean the text

        Lower-case, remove odd symbols. Words like “on”, “off” and “in” stay, because they change the meaning of a command.

      2. Turn words into numbers

        TF-IDF over words and character groups, so “alrm” still looks a lot like “alarm”.

      3. Pick the intent

        Logistic regression scores all 15 intents. Below 45% confidence the app says it is not sure.

      4. Pull out the details

        Rules find times, durations, contacts, places, songs and numbers in the sentence.

      5. Act

        The intent and details become a link, a timer, a device switch or a reply.
